Questions?

Will it run on my Mac?

You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 15 or later and at least 8 GB of memory. Check under Apple menu, About This Mac. Intel Macs are not supported. The download is under 100 MB; installed, Pippa takes about 0.5 GB. She picks the AI that fits your Mac and asks before downloading it: 3 to 17 GB, depending on memory. If LM Studio, Ollama or the Hugging Face cache already has the same model file, she uses it instead of downloading it again.

Can I start without the AI download?

Yes. Turning photos and PDFs into one PDF, making files smaller and converting images work right after you install her, and so does tidying a folder. For letters and questions she loads her AI, but only after your OK, and she tells you the size first.

Do I need internet?

To download the app and her AI, for updates and for web lookups. After that, her AI and the PDF tools work without a connection. If a command needs the internet, she asks you first. An online AI, if you turn one on, needs the internet, of course.

Can I keep using ChatGPT?

Yes. Sign in with ChatGPT in Pippa or enter your own key from OpenAI or Anthropic, and she uses that service as her online AI. While that is on, your conversations go to it. You can turn it off any time in Settings.

What can Pippa access on my Mac?

macOS asks you before she can reach your Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Calendar, Reminders or Photos, or control Mail. You can review and change these permissions in System Settings under Privacy & Security. Pippa runs outside Apple’s App Sandbox so she can work with your folders and Mac apps; a sandboxed app couldn’t do that in full.

What if Pippa gets something wrong?

It happens: AI can miss details, so check important amounts and dates against the original. Tidying shows you the plan first, and Undo works as long as you haven’t changed those files elsewhere. If something goes wrong, report it on GitHub: describe the steps, but leave private documents out.
