Quick ask with ⌥ Space

On a Mac without a notch, Pippa Island hangs under her menu bar icon. Web sources fold into a compact link, and conversations pick up short titles automatically. You can turn the island off under General.

Mail, Calendar, Notes, Finder.

Deadlines go to Reminders, appointments to Calendar, replies to Mail as drafts. Pippa updates existing events and reminders with Undo, sorts emails into your folders and calculates sums and deadlines reliably.

Calendar and Reminders sync through your accounts, such as iCloud. When a web page needs clicking through, she asks first and works in a browser panel in her window.

Questions?

Plain answers, no fine print.

Will it run on my Mac?

You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. Intel Macs are not supported. The download is under 100 MB. Her AI on your Mac needs at least 16 GB of memory and asks before downloading: about 5.4 GB on 16 or 24 GB Macs, or about 13.4 GB on Macs with 32 GB or more. If oMLX, LM Studio or the Hugging Face cache already has the same model, she uses it. With less memory or if you prefer, Pippa uses an online AI. One PDF from photos always works, without any AI.

Can I start without the AI download?

Yes, for one PDF: photos, scans and PDFs become one PDF with One PDF right after you install her. For letters, questions and everything else, she loads her AI only after your OK and tells you the size first.

Do I need internet?

To download the app and her AI, for updates and for web lookups. After that, her AI and the PDF tools work without a connection. If a command needs the internet, she asks you first in the conversation, for each command.

Can I keep using ChatGPT?

Yes. Choose a provider in Pippa’s Settings under AI, sign in or enter an API key, and choose a model. Providers come from the bundled Pi version, including Anthropic, OpenAI/ChatGPT, OpenRouter, OpenCode and xAI. You can turn it off any time.

What can Pippa access on my Mac?

macOS asks you before she can reach your folders, Calendar, Reminders, Photos, Contacts or Mail. Notes, Contacts and Safari tabs work without Full Disk Access. Pippa runs outside Apple’s App Sandbox so she can work with your folders and Mac apps; a sandboxed app couldn’t do that in full.

What if Pippa gets something wrong?

AI can miss details, so check important amounts and dates against the original. Pippa never overwrites a file, anything she removes goes to the Trash, and Undo takes changes back at once. If something goes wrong, open a GitHub issue without private documents.
