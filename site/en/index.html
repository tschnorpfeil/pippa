Pippa’s AI runs on your Mac.

No Pippa account, no Pippa server, no tracking in the app. She goes online only where she has to.

  • ChatGPT only if you want.

    Sign in with ChatGPT, or add your own OpenAI or Anthropic key. While it’s on, your conversations with Pippa go to that service.

  • She never sends your mail.

    Replies wait as unsent drafts in Apple Mail, and events go into Calendar. Those apps may sync through your accounts, such as iCloud.

  • Online only where needed.

    For the AI download, updates, web lookups and commands that need the internet. She asks before a web search and before such a command.

The details are in the privacy policy (German).

Any questions?

Will it run on my Mac?

You need a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later), macOS 15 or later and at least 8 GB of memory. Check under Apple menu, About This Mac. Intel Macs are not supported. The download is under 100 MB; installed, Pippa takes about 0.5 GB. Pippa picks the AI that fits your Mac and asks before downloading it: about 3 GB with 8 GB of memory, about 6 GB with 16 or 18 GB, about 17 GB with 24 GB or more. If LM Studio, Ollama or the Hugging Face cache already has the same model file, she uses it instead of downloading it again. Leave some room for your results, too.

Can I start without the AI download?

Yes. Turning photos and PDFs into one PDF, making files smaller and converting images work right after you install her, and so does tidying a folder. For letters and questions she loads her AI, but only after your OK, and she tells you the size first.

Do I need internet?

To download the app and her AI, for updates and for web lookups. After that, her AI and the PDF tools work without a connection. If a command needs the internet, she asks you first. An online AI, if you turn one on, needs the internet, of course.

What can Pippa access on my Mac?

macOS asks you before she can reach your Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Calendar, Reminders or Photos, or control Mail. You can review and change these permissions in System Settings under Privacy & Security. Pippa runs outside Apple’s App Sandbox so she can work with your folders and Mac apps; a sandboxed app could do that only in part.

What if Pippa gets something wrong?

It happens: AI can miss details, so check important amounts and dates against the original. Tidying shows you the plan first, and Undo works as long as you haven’t changed those files elsewhere. When you ask for a specific action, she carries it out, so look at the result. Pippa keeps getting better, and updates arrive inside the app. If something goes wrong, report it on GitHub: describe the steps, but leave private documents out.
