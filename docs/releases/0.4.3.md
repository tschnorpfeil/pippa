# Pippa 0.4.3

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.4.3**

- **A clearer place to start.** In a new conversation, the message box sits directly beneath Pippa's greeting. The chat opens with more room, and the extra information panel stays closed until you choose to open it.
- **See the files you add.** Photos and PDFs show small previews. Click an attachment to preview it before asking Pippa about it.
- **Your finished PDF is easier to use.** Preview it directly in the conversation, open it, or show it in Finder.
- **Drag and drop works again.** Move the floating Pippa button without opening the chat, and drop messages from Mail onto Pippa or the chat. The button stays on screen after you move it.
- **Getting ready while you type.** When you use the AI on this Mac, Pippa starts loading it as you enter your question, so some of the wait can happen while you type.
- **A greeting stays a greeting.** Clearer instructions help Pippa answer everyday questions briefly and avoid reading another app just because it happens to be in front.
