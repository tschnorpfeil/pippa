# Pippa 1.2.0

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB or 24 GB of memory, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB (or about 13.4 GB on Macs with 32 GB or more); smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 1.2.0**

- **For You desk and scheduled routines.** Open **For You** in the sidebar to add routines from starter packs or in plain words (such as an evening preview, weekly review, calendar or reminder radar, or a watched folder). Pippa prepares items quietly in the background — strictly read-only — and lets you open, run, snooze or mark them done whenever you have a minute.
- **Better flow while Pippa works.** **One PDF** now runs alongside an active answer without interrupting it. You can queue a message in another conversation while Pippa finishes elsewhere, send rapid follow-ups together, and dismiss an open confirmation card automatically when your follow-up replaces it.
- **Clearer cards and question history.** Answered questions stay in the chat as a quiet record. Multiple-choice questions show direct options with an inline reply field right on one card, and Mail drafts appear as an expandable preview card with **Copy text** and **Open in Mail** — never sending on its own.
- **Multi-tab browser, sign-in windows, and smoother forms.** Each conversation keeps its own browser tab on your Mac with real sign-in windows inside the panel and an **Open in Pippa’s Browser** option on web links. Forms ask once with every field listed together, plain links on the same allowed site load without extra questions, and Pippa can read long web pages to the end.
- **Clearer online setup and steadier local chats.** Before you connect an online AI, Pippa shows a brief summary of what stays on your Mac and what goes to your chosen provider, marks the recommended option, and explains online hiccups in calm plain language. Long local conversations also recover automatically instead of getting stuck when memory fills up.
