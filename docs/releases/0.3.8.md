# Pippa 0.3.8

<!-- Text for the GitHub release, English only. The two intro paragraphs are the same in every release (see README.md here). -->

Pippa is a small helper on your Mac's screen for everyday paperwork. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her: she explains what a letter wants from you and by when, makes PDFs smaller or combines them, and tidies up folders. The AI runs on your Mac, with no account and no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. On first start she downloads her AI once (about 3 to 6 GB, depending on your Mac's memory).

**New in Pippa 0.3.8**

- **Permissions don't get lost anymore.** "What Pippa may do" now also appears when you put off loading the AI. If you close the page, it comes back once more at a later start. Open items have a blue "Allow" button, and next to "Continue" Pippa says how many are still open.
- **Plain words for blocked folders.** If your Mac didn't allow Pippa a folder, she says so when tidying and takes you to System Settings with one click.
- **Tidying with visible progress.** While Pippa looks at your files, the "Organize" button itself shows how far she is ("Reading 12 of 42…"), and next to it a line says which file she is reading or whether she is recognizing the text of a scan.
- **Tidying uses folders that are already there.** If there is a "Documents" folder already, Pippa doesn't create a second "Dokumente" next to it. A file that is already in its place keeps its name.
