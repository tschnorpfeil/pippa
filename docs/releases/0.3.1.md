# Pippa 0.3.1

<!-- Text for the GitHub release, English only. The two intro paragraphs are the same in every release (see README.md here). The full 0.3 notes follow below them. -->

Pippa is a small helper on your Mac's screen for everyday paperwork. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her: she explains what a letter wants from you and by when, makes PDFs smaller or combines them, and tidies up folders. The AI runs on your Mac, with no account and no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. On first start she downloads her AI once (about 3 to 6 GB, depending on your Mac's memory).

**New in Pippa 0.3.1**

- **Pippa's handwriting while she answers.** While Pippa answers, you see her writing in her own hand.
- **A calmer pill.** Six clear states instead of eight show what Pippa is doing.
- **Tables are checked.** For Excel, CSV and Numbers files, Pippa works out sums and comparisons with code instead of guessing.
- **Invoices and letters.** Invoices become an Excel sheet with a total, and unsure values are marked in yellow. "As Letter" turns a draft into a letter as a PDF or Word file.
