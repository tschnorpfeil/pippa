# Pippa 0.3.15

Pippa is a private AI agent that lives on your Mac. Ask her something, or drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, and she does the work: she explains what a letter wants from you and by when, finds files, drafts replies in Mail and tidies up folders. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat, Pippa asks before downloading about 3 to 6 GB, depending on your Mac's memory.

**New in Pippa 0.3.15**

- **An updated Pi core.** Pippa now includes Pi 1.4.0, with upstream fixes for continuing conversations after interrupted tool calls, restoring extension tools in saved sessions and handling empty tool output.
- **More resilient long conversations.** Pi improves conversation summaries and recovery from temporary service errors. Pippa keeps using Pi's agent loop for multi-step work.

Local AI choices, file actions and the optional morning overview are unchanged. This release updates the bundled Pi installation automatically with Pippa; you do not need Terminal.

Validation includes real Pi integration and installer upgrade checks, macOS CI, signed and notarized packaging, and an actual Sparkle update from Pippa 0.3.14. Physical 8 GB and 16 GB Macs and a fresh macOS permission profile were unavailable. Calendar/Reminders checks use synthetic readers; personal account and Mail workflows are not newly certified. A short local AI smoke test is not a general planning or success-rate benchmark. See `validation.json` below for the final acceptance record.
