# Pippa 0.3.11

<!-- Text for the GitHub release, English only. The two intro paragraphs are the same in every release (see README.md here). -->

Pippa is a private AI agent that lives on your Mac. Ask her something, or drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, and she does the work: she explains what a letter wants from you and by when, finds files, drafts replies in Mail and tidies up folders, and she asks before she changes anything. The AI runs on your Mac, so your files stay with you: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. On first start she downloads her AI once (about 3 to 6 GB, depending on your Mac's memory).

**New in Pippa 0.3.11**

- **"What do you think of this?" now means the page in front of you.** When the app in front is attached to your question and you ask about "this", "this page" or "summarize that", Pippa reads the open page or the selected mail herself before she answers. If your Mac doesn't allow her to read it yet, she tells you what to allow instead of saying she can't see it.
- **Under the hood.** Pippa's assistant moved to a newer version. When a step comes out garbled, Pippa now notices and tries again, instead of carrying it out halfway.
