# Pippa 0.3.10

<!-- Text for the GitHub release, English only. The two intro paragraphs are the same in every release (see README.md here). -->

Pippa is a small helper on your Mac's screen for everyday paperwork. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her: she explains what a letter wants from you and by when, makes PDFs smaller or combines them, and tidies up folders. The AI runs on your Mac, with no account and no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. On first start she downloads her AI once (about 3 to 6 GB, depending on your Mac's memory).

**New in Pippa 0.3.10**

- **The AI download no longer gets stuck at the end.** On a shaky internet connection the first download could stop at 100 % and never finish. Pippa now finishes as soon as everything has arrived, and picks up again by herself when the connection goes quiet.
- **No more "100 %" before Pippa is ready.** Once the download is in, Pippa says she is checking and setting up her AI, instead of showing 100 % while there is still work to do.
- **"What Pippa may do" no longer hangs.** A permission row could keep spinning for minutes, and Pippa then refused to quit. Each row now waits at most a short while, and if your Mac doesn't answer, the row offers "Try Again". Quitting Pippa always works.
- **Under the hood.** Pippa's assistant moved to a newer version, with smaller summaries of long conversations and steadier connections to Pippa's own tools.
