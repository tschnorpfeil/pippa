# Pippa 0.6.3

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.6.3**

- **The open Pippa Island stays left of the notch.** Opened, the island no longer reaches over the menu bar right of the notch, where tools like tinyStats live. Her mark and name stay up in the menu bar row beside the notch instead of moving down a line, which leaves more room below.
- **Pippa's handwriting while she writes.** While the words of an answer come in, her mark turns into flowing handwriting, on Pippa Island and in the chat window.
- **See how Pippa gets there.** While she works, the open island shows her steps as a chain. At its end you see what she is thinking right now, or the answer as she writes it.
- **The question field keeps the cursor.** After you pressed Return in Pippa Island, the field lost the cursor while she answered. Now you can keep typing your next question right away.
