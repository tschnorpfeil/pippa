# Pippa 0.6.1

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.6.1**

- **An almost black window in Dark Mode.** The chat window, the conversation list and About this answer are now nearly black, like Pippa Island at the top of your screen. Light Mode looks as before.
- **A faster first download.** Pippa fetches her AI over several connections at once. If that fails, she carries on over one connection, as before.
- **A plain sentence when your Mac is short on memory.** When other apps leave too little memory for the AI mid-answer, Pippa now says so and suggests closing a few apps, instead of showing a technical message.
- **Calmer steps under an answer.** Opening and closing what Pippa did no longer slides over the answer, and the answer no longer jumps down when Pippa finishes while the steps are open.
- **No wide scroll bar.** With a mouse connected, the conversation showed a wide scroll bar. It is gone.
- **Copy stays on one line.** In a narrow window, the Copy button under an answer was squeezed onto two lines by the sources beside it.
