# Pippa 0.6.5

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.6.5**

- **One look for Pippa Island, the chat window and Settings.** The same rounded buttons, the same calm surfaces and one blue for the thing you are meant to click.
- **A calmer Pippa Island.** When Pippa asks for permission, the question sits right on the island with one clear **Allow** button. The question field and **Open Pippa** share one row, the place to drop files is a quiet tray, and the island grows and shrinks smoothly instead of jumping.
- **Answers that are easier to read.** Headings stand out from the text, tables are quieter, and file names read as part of the sentence.
- **Which AI answers is named once.** The chat window shows it next to the question field, by its name instead of a technical ID. If you use an online AI, choose it once more in **Settings** → **AI** to see the name.
- **Settings match the chat.** Almost black in Dark Mode, with one set of text sizes and the names of the online AIs instead of their IDs.
