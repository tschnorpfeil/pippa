# Pippa 0.3.9

Text for the GitHub release, in English.

**Pippa is a small pill on your Mac: drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on it and pick a step, like explaining a letter, making one PDF from photos or tidying your Downloads. The AI runs on your Mac. It needs a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. Download `Pippa.dmg` below; on first start Pippa offers to load her AI (3 to 6 GB).**

## What's new in 0.3.9

- **"What Pippa may do" stays in view.** After you allow something, Pippa brings her window back to the front, in the Welcome as well as in Settings. Before, it often ended up behind other windows and looked closed.
- **The permissions page no longer gets lost.** Until you press "Continue" or ×, clicking Pippa takes you back to "What Pippa may do".
- **Tidying without waiting for the AI.** Apple's model on your Mac now places unclear documents; otherwise they go to "Documents" by their kind. Tidying no longer waits for Pippa's own AI to load.
