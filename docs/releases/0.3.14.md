# Pippa 0.3.14

Pippa is a private AI agent that lives on your Mac. Ask her something, or drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, and she does the work: she explains what a letter wants from you and by when, finds files, drafts replies in Mail and tidies up folders. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat, Pippa asks before downloading about 3 to 6 GB, depending on your Mac's memory.

**New in Pippa 0.3.14**

- **Get files ready to send.** Ask in chat, or give Pippa PDFs or photos and choose “Ready to send.” Pippa prepares one PDF in the requested order, checks its pages and reports its actual size. The tray also lets you choose quality and a size limit before starting. Your originals stay unchanged. You choose where to save or send the result.
- **Pick up a saved file task.** Requests and verified results survive an app restart. Interrupted work waits for you to review and continue. A failed Save keeps the result available; “Saved File Tasks…” brings unfinished requests back.
- **An optional morning overview.** Choose the calendars and reminder lists you want checked, plus days and a time. Pippa puts today's appointments and due tasks in a local overview, with links back to their source apps. Missing access is shown explicitly. Optional notifications contain no appointment or task details.

The morning overview starts off. It works while Pippa and your Mac are running, catches up once within four hours of the chosen time and does not replay missed days. Overdue reminders cover the previous 30 days. The tray action and morning overview work without loading the chat AI, including on macOS 15. In chat, Pippa can use these file actions as part of a longer request.
