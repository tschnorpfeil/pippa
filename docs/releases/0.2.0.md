# Pippa 0.2

<!-- Text for the GitHub release, English only. The two intro paragraphs are the same in every release (see README.md here). -->

Pippa is a small helper on your Mac's screen for everyday paperwork. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her: she explains what a letter wants from you and by when, makes PDFs smaller or combines them, and tidies up folders. The AI runs on your Mac, with no account and no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. On first start she downloads her AI once (about 3 to 6 GB, depending on your Mac's memory).

**New in Pippa 0.2**

- **A new AI on your Mac.** Macs with 16 GB or more now get Qwen3.5 9B. Pippa removes the old AI file by herself after its first answer.
- **Start right away.** While the AI is still loading, Apple Intelligence on your Mac already answers simple questions. Anything that needs your files, Mail or the web waits in the conversation and goes out by itself as soon as Pippa's AI is ready. If you opened Pippa from the download window, she offers once to move herself to Applications.
- **Pippa remembers what matters.** Tell her where you live, who your doctor or your landlord is, and she knows it next time. "Forget that" is enough to remove it. New topics start by themselves, no button needed, and carry over what matters, such as deadlines and amounts.
- **Answers in your language.** Ask in English and Pippa answers in English, even when the document is in German.
- **Faster.** The first answer arrives about a third sooner, text appears more smoothly, and the pill stays responsive while Pippa reads Mail or Excel.
- **The pill says what's happening.** For example "Reading the lease · 2/4". A finished answer stays on the pill until you open it.
- **ChatGPT if you like.** With a ChatGPT subscription you can sign in from Settings and use it instead of the AI on your Mac.

For Macs with Apple silicon and macOS 15 or later. Download **Pippa.dmg**, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Signed with Developer ID and notarized by Apple. If you already have Pippa, the update arrives automatically.
