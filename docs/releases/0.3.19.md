# Pippa 0.3.19

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat, Pippa asks before downloading about 3 to 17 GB, depending on your Mac's memory.

**New in Pippa 0.3.19**

- **She starts thinking sooner again.** In 0.3.18 Pippa often skipped warming up her AI when you opened her, then had to load it after you asked. She now warms up as soon as you open her again, and only holds back when your Mac is critically short on memory.
- **The conversation stays in front again.** The open conversation floats above your other apps as it did before 0.3.18, and Settings opens above it. The Float on Top pin is gone.
- **"Do I have new mail?" works.** Pippa can now list your newest or unread emails without guessing a search word.
- **A shorter tool list.** Pippa reads the selected email and the cells selected in Excel through the app you came from, and no longer carries a text-replacement tool meant for code. Her AI has three fewer tools to read before each new start.
