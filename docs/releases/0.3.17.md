# Pippa 0.3.17

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat, Pippa asks before downloading about 3 to 17 GB, depending on your Mac's memory.

**New in Pippa 0.3.17**

- **Your conversations and results beside each other.** Browse, rename and delete conversations from the side rail. Files, drafts, charts and sources stay within reach in the details rail; hide either rail when you need more room.
- **A clearer first start.** One action starts the AI download, interrupted downloads can resume, and Stop Loading pauses it. Pippa checks free space and shows a ready step with the shortcut and Open at Login.
- **Charts from your tables.** Ask for a bar, line or pie chart from a spreadsheet or CSV. Pippa calculates the values from the file and offers Save as Picture and Copy Picture, including any note about incomplete data.
- **Remember useful steps.** Ask Pippa to save a reusable workflow for later conversations. She shows the steps and asks before saving. Cancelled requests leave nothing saved, and existing workflows are kept.
- **A refreshed website and clearer guides.** The English and German pages explain the results Pippa can make, with more detail about privacy, permissions and local AI in the documentation.
