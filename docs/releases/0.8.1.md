# Pippa 0.8.1

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.8.1**

- **Let a trusted online AI work with your Mac.** In Settings under **AI → Sharing with online AI**, **Allow Mac access** (off by default) lets the online AI you signed in to use Pippa's Mac tools directly, tied to that exact provider and account. Pippa's confirmations and macOS permissions still apply, and turning it off or switching accounts ends access right away.
- **Better Mail triage across accounts.** Pippa reads each Mail account separately, names any account that did not answer, checks unread or read mail as well as Sent and Drafts so she does not suggest replying to messages you already answered, handles larger inboxes in pages, and saves unsent reply drafts in the right thread.
- **Richer Calendar events and Reminders.** Calendar readings now include organizers, attendees, links and notes, and Reminders include notes and priority. Pippa can add multi-day events (up to 31 days) and place an event in a calendar you name.
- **Type a web address in Pippa's browser.** When the browser panel is open and empty, you can enter a web address directly without colliding with Pippa's own browsing steps.
- **Quieter Settings and smoother everyday use.** When online AI is off, the sharing rules collapse into one summary row so AI Settings stay uncluttered, and online setup explains sharing once instead of twice. Calendar times and setup error buttons no longer clip, stopping a download or PDF job settles cleanly, and typing, dragging files and Pippa Island updates stay responsive.
