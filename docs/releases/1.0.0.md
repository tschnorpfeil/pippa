# Pippa 1.0.0

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 1.0.0**

- **Pippa gets on with it.** What she can take back, she now does without asking: adding an event or a reminder, saving an unsent draft, moving files, moving mails into your folders. This holds for the AI on your Mac and for an online AI you gave **Allow Mac access**.
- **One Undo for the whole answer.** Under each answer one line says what Pippa did and what she checked in Mail, Calendar, Reminders or your files. **Undo** takes all of it back and leaves alone what you changed since. If she was interrupted, the line says "unclear, please check", and she does not do that step again. Pippa keeps this record for 30 days.
- **Sort your inbox into your folders.** Ask her to tidy your inbox, and Pippa moves mails into the folders you already have in that account. She checks each mail in its new folder and stops if one does not show up. She never creates folders and never moves mail into Trash, Junk, Sent or Drafts. For a big inbox, use an online AI with **Allow Mac access**. The AI on your Mac is not yet up to sorting hundreds of mails.
- **Send a draft with one click.** Pippa can send a reply draft she saved in the conversation. A card shows recipient, subject and text, and the mail goes out only when you press **Send**. She sends nothing else.
- **Questions only where they matter.** Pippa asks before anything that leaves your Mac or cannot be taken back. A command that needs the internet now asks each time instead of once per answer.
- **A busy online AI is waited out.** When it says "too many requests", Pippa waits, shows the countdown and continues. If it stays busy, press **Continue** after a minute. When your allowance with that provider is used up, Pippa says so instead.
