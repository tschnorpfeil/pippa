# Pippa 0.6.2

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.6.2**

- **Pippa says when her AI is still starting.** After a restart or a pause, the AI on your Mac loads into memory before the first answer. The chat window and Pippa Island now say "Starting my AI…" during that time instead of leaving you guessing. An info button beside it explains in a few sentences what is happening.
- **"Writing…" only while Pippa writes.** Pippa showed "Writing…" as soon as an answer held any text, even while she was still thinking or reading. Now it shows only while words are coming in.
- **Drop files on Pippa Island.** Dragging a file from another app onto the open island closed it the moment you started dragging. Now the file lands on the island, and a plain click elsewhere still closes it.
- **Call Pippa with ⌥ Space.** The shortcut is now Option-Space. If you chose a different shortcut in Settings, Pippa keeps it. While ⌥ Space calls Pippa, it no longer types a non-breaking space; pick ⌃⌥ Space in Settings if you need that.
- **Pippa Island keeps to the left of the notch.** Her blue mark now sits just left of the notch, also while she rests, and what she is doing appears there too. The right side stays free for your menu bar icons and other notch tools.
