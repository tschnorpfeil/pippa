# Pippa 0.3.16

Pippa is a private AI agent that lives on your Mac. Ask her something, or drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, and she does the work: she explains what a letter wants from you and by when, finds files, drafts replies in Mail and tidies up folders. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat, Pippa asks before downloading about 3 to 17 GB, depending on your Mac's memory.

**New in Pippa 0.3.16**

- **An updated local AI for Macs with 24 GB or more.** The new setup uses the configuration selected in our document and everyday-task checks. Macs with less memory keep their smaller download.
- **Less overhead for document questions.** Pippa starts straightforward reading and comparison tasks with a smaller set of tools, and can bring in the rest when needed.
- **Better searches across German and English folders.** Invoice and insurance searches also recognize the corresponding folder names in the other language, while keeping your other search terms and year.
- **Remember your local AI selection.** An explicitly configured local choice survives app restarts.

Local AI can still miss details or make factual mistakes. Check important amounts, dates and conclusions against the original documents.
