# Pippa 0.6.0

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.6.0**

- **A more reliable first start.** Asking a question while setup finishes no longer interrupts the installation. Pippa also repairs her own incomplete AI installation when files are missing.
- **Open the email you meant.** Inbox results select the newest matching emails before limiting the list. Emails with the same subject can be opened individually, and the source beneath an answer opens the exact message in Mail.
- **Keep document attachments.** PDFs and CSV files displayed inside an email are kept as readable attachments. Missing attachment content is identified, so a filename alone is no longer treated as a readable file.
- **Check nearby dates too.** Dates that are not in what Pippa read are flagged even when they are only a few days away.

Pippa can still choose an outdated document or misinterpret a deadline measured from receipt. Check the original before acting on those answers.
