# Pippa 0.7.0

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.7.0**

- **Pippa's browser.** When a web page needs clicking through, Pippa opens it in a browser panel in her window, so you can watch. She asks before each new website and before every click, typed text or choice. Sign-ins, passwords, codes and "I'm not a robot" checks stay with you; she never asks for your password. The globe button in the window's toolbar shows or hides the panel.
- **Keep asking on Pippa Island.** Click your last conversation on Pippa Island, or press ↑ in the empty field, and your next question goes on with it, without opening the window. The pencil (⌘N) starts a new conversation. A question also goes on by itself while her answer is showing or the conversation's last message is under ten minutes old.
- **Reads more apps in front.** Ask about what's open and Pippa now also reads apps that can't hand over their content any other way, through macOS Accessibility. She only reads the visible text, never clicks or types there, skips password fields, and explains this before macOS asks you.
- **Safer commands.** Commands Pippa runs on your Mac can no longer reach other apps' background services or borrow Pippa's Accessibility access.
- **Fixes.** Pippa asks with choices instead of listing options in her text, and her handwriting shows again in the menu bar while she writes.
