# Pippa 0.6.6

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.6.6**

- **Long documents, read to the end.** A thesis or a long Word file no longer gets answered from its first pages. Pippa reads it part by part, shows "Part 3 of 20" while she does, and keeps notes on all of it, so a second question about the same file is answered right away.
- **Pippa Island says each thing once.** Her mark shows in one place only, and what she is doing appears in one line: no second mark, no "Pippa is answering…" under "Writing…". The question field reads **Next question…** while she works.
- **The island stays when you hide the chat.** Hiding Pippa with ⌘H puts the chat window away; the island beside the notch stays, like a menu bar icon.
- **One live mark in the menu bar.** On a Mac with a notch the island shows what Pippa is doing and the menu bar icon stays calm; on other Macs the menu bar icon does it.
- **Answers you have seen stay seen.** Once you have read an answer, the island starts empty again, with your latest conversation one click away.
