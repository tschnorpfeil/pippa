# Pippa 1.1.0

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB or 24 GB of memory, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB (or about 13.4 GB on Macs with 32 GB or more); smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 1.1.0**

- **Calm notices (opt-in).** Switched off until you turn it on under Settings › General › Calm notices. Pippa shows at most two quiet notices a day in Pippa Island (never stealing focus, never waking her built-in AI in the background), respects quiet hours and a locked screen, and lets you open a notice in chat, mark it done, snooze it, or mute that kind in one click.
- **Morning brief without waiting.** Pick a morning hour and Pippa puts together today’s appointments, due reminders and open items directly from the Calendar and Reminders access you already granted — staying silent on an empty day. You can preview today’s brief right in Settings.
- **Watched folder for new paperwork.** Choose one folder (such as Downloads or a scan folder) and Pippa watches its top level for new letters, bills or deadlines, ignoring incomplete downloads and unchanged files, and hands the file to your chat only when you click Open in Chat.
- **Reliable Check for Updates button.** When an update has already been downloaded in the background, Settings › Updates now shows Install and Restart and the Pippa menu shows Restart to Update instead of ignoring Check for Updates…, and asks first if Pippa is answering right now.
- **Redesigned website and social preview cards.** Updated heypippa.app (German and English) with the live six-scene window and matching link preview cards.
