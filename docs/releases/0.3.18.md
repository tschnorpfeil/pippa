# Pippa 0.3.18

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat, Pippa asks before downloading about 3 to 17 GB, depending on your Mac's memory.

**New in Pippa 0.3.18**

- **Lighter on your Mac's memory.** Pippa keeps her AI within a fixed memory budget and steps back when your Mac runs short, even while the AI is still starting. She no longer warms up in the background when memory is tight, the Mac is hot or Low Power Mode is on, and if memory is too low to start she says so plainly.
- **Faster answers on Macs with 24 GB.** These Macs now use the same lighter AI as 16 GB Macs. It answered Pippa's everyday tasks at least as well in our tests, in about two thirds of the time, with less than half the memory. Pippa asks once before the smaller download (about 6 GB); the previous files stay on your Mac until you delete them.
- **The conversation can step back.** The open conversation now sits behind other apps like any window. Turn on Float on Top in its header to keep it in front.
- **See what Pippa is doing.** Each step shows how long it took, the working sign shows whether she is thinking, reading or writing, and Show Reasoning reveals her reasoning for an answer.
- **Stop Answering.** Stop a long answer at any time from the conversation.
