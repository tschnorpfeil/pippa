# Pippa 0.8.0

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.8.0**

- **Choose where Pippa answers.** On first launch, Pippa shows the size of the download for your Mac and offers an online option in the same window. If your Mac cannot run the local AI, the online option comes first. After you sign in, a separate button names the provider you can use. You can already drop photos or scans to make a PDF.
- **Choose what each AI can use.** In Settings under **AI**, you can turn web search and personal notes on or off separately for the AI on your Mac and your online AI. Changes apply from your next question. Your saved notes stay on your Mac when you turn the feature off.
- **Pick up the conversation from Pippa Island.** When the Island shows an answer or a question, **Open Pippa** opens that conversation and keeps your draft.
- **Drop two messages with the same file name.** Pippa keeps both when they are different emails.
