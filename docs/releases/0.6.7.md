# Pippa 0.6.7

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.6.7**

- **Follow-up questions on Pippa Island.** Ask "And what about chapter 6?" right after an answer and Pippa remembers what you were talking about. The field says **Follow up…**; the pencil next to it (⌘N) starts something new instead.
- **A calm Pippa Island while she works.** Her steps no longer make the island grow and shrink with every step, and her thinking stays out of the island: it says **Thinking…** with the seconds. The answer appears in its last three lines without jumping.
- **Which AI answers, right on the island.** A small laptop or cloud next to the question field shows whether Pippa answers on this Mac or online; point at it for the name, click it to change it in **Settings**.
- **Room to breathe in the chat.** An answer no longer runs into the question field below it.
