# Pippa 0.5.0

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.5.0**

- **A new look for macOS Tahoe.** The chat window, the conversation list and Settings use the new Liquid Glass design. Pippa's answers appear smoothly as she writes, also long ones.
- **Pippa Island.** Pippa now lives at the top of your screen: at the notch on a MacBook, or under her symbol in the menu bar. It shows what she is working on, and Pippa's keyboard shortcut opens it for a quick question.
- **Questions right in the conversation.** When Pippa needs your OK, for example before she searches the web, she asks in the chat with **Allow** and **Don't Allow** instead of a separate window.
- **Settings with pages.** **General**, **AI**, **Permissions** and **Updates**. The new **Permissions** page shows at a glance what Pippa may use on this Mac and takes you to the right place in System Settings.
- **Easier start on a new Mac.** Before Pippa downloads her AI, she checks that your Mac has enough free space and tells you how much to free up. If the AI on your Mac runs short of memory, Pippa says so in one plain sentence. And Pippa never brings up Apple's "install developer tools" window.
- **A second source for the AI download.** If the usual download of Pippa's AI is blocked on your network or keeps failing, she now carries on from a copy in Pippa's releases on GitHub, on her own. She still checks every file before she uses it, so nothing else can slip in.
- **Faster writing with the AI on your Mac.** Pippa's AI engine now uses extra speed-ups built for Apple silicon. On a Mac mini with M6 she writes her answers about 20 % faster; older chips may gain less. If you already use the AI on your Mac, Pippa fetches this improvement once after the update, about 350 MB, and removes the old version.
- **macOS 26.2 or later.** Pippa 0.5.0 needs macOS 26.2 (Tahoe) or later. On an older macOS, Pippa 0.4.5 keeps working but doesn't offer this update; once you update macOS in **System Settings** › **General** › **Software Update**, Pippa offers it.
