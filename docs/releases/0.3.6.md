# Pippa 0.3.6

<!-- Text for the GitHub release, English only. The two intro paragraphs are the same in every release (see README.md here). The 0.3.5 and 0.3.1 notes and the full 0.3 notes follow below them. -->

Pippa is a small helper on your Mac's screen for everyday paperwork. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her: she explains what a letter wants from you and by when, makes PDFs smaller or combines them, and tidies up folders. The AI runs on your Mac, with no account and no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. On first start she downloads her AI once (about 3 to 6 GB, depending on your Mac's memory).

**New in Pippa 0.3.6**

- **Whole thought bubbles.** When an answer takes longer, the sentence in the thought bubble at the pill is now shown in full and no longer cut off in the middle.
- **Finding files.** Pippa now also finds files by their name or the name of the folder they are in, across the whole home folder, not just Documents, Desktop and Downloads.
- **Found files as a card.** When Pippa names files, they appear as a card you can click, also when Pippa opened a folder. Each row has a visible "Show in Finder" button ("Im Finder zeigen").
