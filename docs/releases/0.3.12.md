# Pippa 0.3.12

<!-- Release candidate notes. Publish only with the matching verified, signed package and appcast. -->

Pippa is a private AI agent that lives on your Mac. Ask her something, or drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, and she does the work: she explains what a letter wants from you and by when, finds files, drafts replies in Mail and tidies up folders, and she asks before she changes anything. The AI runs on your Mac, so your files stay with you: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. On first start she downloads her AI once (about 3 to 6 GB, depending on your Mac's memory).

**New in Pippa 0.3.12**

- **Get useful work done before the AI download.** Start with photos or PDFs directly from setup. Open the finished file, show it in Finder, save it elsewhere or undo the result. If saving fails, the result stays available.
- **More dependable Mac actions.** Pippa checks that her Mac tools are connected before answering. Reminder creation now keeps a local record of the requested action and its destination. After reopening, Pippa checks identifiable unfinished reminders and keeps their status with the original conversation. An unconfirmed reminder blocks another reminder in that conversation until Pippa can verify the earlier result.
- **Safer recovery.** Errors and Stop preserve available file backups. Pippa distinguishes a blocked command from an attempt that may already have changed something. A permission grant no longer automatically repeats a partly completed command. Try Again retains the original task and checks whether its attached source has changed.
- **Drafts stay focused on drafting.** Reply and letter drafting use a restricted set of reading tools. Commands, file changes and changes in other apps are unavailable during these tasks. General app control through commands has been replaced by Pippa's dedicated Mac tools.
- **More everyday shortcuts.** Extract text from PDFs and images, remove location metadata from a photo, fix spelling or turn a short selection into a checklist. Eligible small text actions use Apple's on-device intelligence when available.
