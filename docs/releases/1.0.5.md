# Pippa 1.0.5

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB or 24 GB of memory, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB (or about 13.4 GB on Macs with 32 GB or more); smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 1.0.5**

- **Stronger built-in AI on 32 GB+ Macs.** Macs with 32 GB of memory or more now run Inkling 2 Mini on an updated local AI engine, including direct reading of photos and screenshots. Macs with 16 GB or 24 GB continue with Ornith 1.5 9B, and once an upgraded Mac finishes setting up the new built-in AI, the older files move to the Trash automatically.
- **Follow up while Pippa works.** You can send a short correction or follow-up directly in the chat while Pippa is still working, without pressing Stop and starting over.
- **Livelier chat and clearer steps.** A shimmering status line names the specific step Pippa is taking, a progress ring frames Stop, and web sources fold into a compact source link.
- **Notes, Contacts, Safari tabs, Calendar and Reminders updates, and exact calculations.** Pippa can read Apple Notes, look up Contacts, check open Safari tabs, update Calendar events and Reminders with Undo, and calculate sums, dates and deadlines reliably — all without Full Disk Access.
- **Smarter conversation titles.** Conversations pick up short titles automatically using Apple Intelligence where it is enabled, with a clean fallback otherwise.
