# Pippa 1.0.1

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 26.2 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 1.0.1**

- **Reading web pages works again with the AI on your Mac.** Sometimes the built-in AI got the name of its page reader slightly wrong, tried it four times, and then answered from search snippets alone. Pippa now recognizes what it meant and reads the page.
- **One question per conversation for web search.** Pippa asks before her first web search in a conversation and shows you the exact search text. After your yes, later searches in the same conversation go ahead. A new conversation asks again, and a no holds only for that search.
- **Clearer steps.** A web address shows as "Reading a web page" instead of a file name, and a step Pippa cannot name says "Using a tool" instead of a technical name.
- **Updated built-in Pi engine (1.6.0).** Includes the latest provider list and fixes for retries after dropped connections and for tool calls with some online AIs.
