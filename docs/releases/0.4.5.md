# Pippa 0.4.5

Pippa is a free Mac helper for paperwork and small chores. Drop a letter, a PDF, a folder or a few photos on her, or ask her something, and she hands back the result: one PDF ready to send, what a letter wants from you and by when, a tidy folder, a reply draft in Mail. Her built-in AI runs on your Mac: no account, no subscription.

**You need** a Mac with Apple silicon (M1 or later) and macOS 15 or later. **Download** `Pippa.dmg` below, open it and drag Pippa to Applications. Photo and PDF actions work before AI setup. For AI chat on a Mac with 16 GB of memory or more, Pippa asks before downloading about 5.4 GB; smaller Macs can use an online AI.

**New in Pippa 0.4.5**

- **Undo for calendar entries and reminders.** When Pippa adds something to Calendar or Reminders, the line under her answer now has an **Undo** button. It removes the entry only if you haven't changed it since; if you have, or it's already gone, Pippa says so and leaves it alone.
- **Sign in with ChatGPT, then just chat.** After you sign in with ChatGPT under **Settings › Online AI**, Pippa uses it right away. Before, she showed "Connected" but kept answering with the AI on your Mac until you picked one of many options and confirmed it.
- **Clearer messages from your online AI.** If your ChatGPT sign-in has expired or you've reached your usage limit, Pippa now tells you in one plain sentence what to do instead of showing a technical error.
- **Tidier Settings and PDF results.** The online AI section lines up with the rest of Settings, and a finished PDF shows as one compact row with **Open** and **Show in Finder** beside it.
- **Less clutter on your Mac.** Older copies of Pippa's helper that are no longer needed are now removed reliably after an update, saving about 150 MB each. Thanks to [@tnalbant](https://github.com/tnalbant) for finding and fixing this (#141).
